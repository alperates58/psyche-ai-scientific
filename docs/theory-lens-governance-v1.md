# PsycheAI FAZ 2.19 — Theory Lens Governance & Safety Framework

## 1. Epistemic Principles

The PsycheAI Theory Lens system operates strictly downstream of the Master Psychological Model (FAZ 2.14–2.17). It provides historical, conceptual, and philosophical lenses through which deterministic measurements can be contextualized.

### Core Governance Rules:

1. **Downstream Exclusivity**:
   Theories must never feed back into psychometric scoring or modify underlying numerical values.

2. **Epistemic Labeling**:
   Every statement generated in the user interface is tagged:
   - `[MEASURED_FINDING]`: Direct reference to psychometric evidence.
   - `[THEORETICAL_INTERPRETATION]`: Theoretical hypothesis or perspective.
   - `[USER_PROVIDED_CONTEXT]`: Information provided by the user in conversation.
   - `[REFLECTIVE_HYPOTHESIS]`: Open-ended reflective question.

3. **No Childhood or Trauma Inferences**:
   Classical psychoanalytic or developmental theories often emphasize early childhood experiences. The AI engine is strictly barred from asserting or speculating about a user's childhood, parental relationships, or traumatic events.

4. **No Clinical Diagnosis**:
   No psychiatric diagnosis (e.g. DSM-5 / ICD-11 categories) may be assigned, suggested, or implied.

5. **No Theory Primacy / Non-Competitive Framing**:
   The engine does not declare one theoretical school "superior" or "objectively true" over others. Historical context and empirical limitations are presented transparently alongside each lens.
