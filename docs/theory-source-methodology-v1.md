## 2. Canonical Source Registry Summary

### 1. Sigmund Freud (`FREUD`)
- *The Ego and the Id (Das Ich und das Es)* (1923). Internationaler Psychoanalytischer Verlag.
- *Instincts and Their Vicissitudes (Triebe und Triebschicksale)* (1915). Internationale Zeitschrift für Ärztliche Psychoanalyse.

### 2. Carl Gustav Jung (`JUNG`)
- *Psychological Types (Psychologische Typen)* (1921). Rascher & Cie.
- *Two Essays on Analytical Psychology* (1928). Routledge & Kegan Paul.

### 3. Alfred Adler (`ADLER`)
- *The Neurotic Constitution (Über den nervösen Charakter)* (1912). J. F. Bergmann.
- *Social Interest: A Challenge to Mankind* (1938). Faber & Faber.

### 4. Carl Rogers (`ROGERS`)
- *Client-Centered Therapy: Its Current Practice, Implications and Theory* (1951). Houghton Mifflin.
- *On Becoming a Person: A Therapist's View of Psychotherapy* (1961). Houghton Mifflin.

### 5. Abraham Maslow (`MASLOW`)
- *A Theory of Human Motivation* (1943). Psychological Review.
- *Toward a Psychology of Being* (1968). D. Van Nostrand.

### 6. B. F. Skinner (`SKINNER`)
- *Science and Human Behavior* (1953). Macmillan.
- *About Behaviorism* (1974). Alfred A. Knopf.

### 7. William James (`WILLIAM_JAMES`)
- *The Principles of Psychology* (1890). Henry Holt and Company.
- *Pragmatism: A New Name for Some Old Ways of Thinking* (1907). Longmans, Green, and Co.

### 8. Gestalt Psychology (`GESTALT`)
- *Principles of Gestalt Psychology* (1935). Harcourt, Brace & World.
- *Gestalt Therapy: Excitement and Growth in the Human Personality* (1951). Julian Press.

### 9. Viktor Frankl (`FRANKL`)
- *Man's Search for Meaning (Ein Psycholog erlebt das Konzentrationslager)* (1946). Verlag Jugend und Volk.
- *The Will to Meaning: Foundations and Applications of Logotherapy* (1969). World Publishing Company.

### 10. Aaron T. Beck (`BECK`)
- *Depression: Clinical, Experimental, and Theoretical Aspects* (1967). Harper & Row.
- *Cognitive Therapy and the Emotional Disorders* (1976). International Universities Press.
