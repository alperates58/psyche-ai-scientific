## Response quality
- Ertuna et al. (2026). Careless responding and effects on reliability, validity and invariance. Front Psychol. PMID 42220343 / PMCID PMC13219303.
- Meade/Curran family of careless-response detection methods: use multiple indicators; no single signal is definitive.

## Historical theory caution
- Wahba & Bridwell (1976), Maslow reconsidered: empirical support for strict need hierarchy is limited/partial. DOI:10.1016/0030-5073(76)90038-6

## Licensing policy
- Do not copy proprietary test item text into production without explicit license.
- IPIP is explicitly public domain according to its official site.
- Rosenberg page currently states public domain; preserve citation and confirm jurisdictional/translation status before commercial launch.
- Translation rights/validation are separate from original instrument rights. A Turkish translation must not be called validated unless evidence supports it.
